# Vendor Management

## Creating a New Vendor Record
1. Go to **Purchasing > Vendors > New Vendor**.
2. Enter Vendor Name, Tax ID, and primary contact details.
3. Assign a Vendor Code (auto-generated or manually entered if permitted).
4. Set payment terms (e.g., Net 30, Net 60).
5. Click **Save**. New vendors default to "Pending Review" status until approved by Finance.

## Vendor Recommendation Engine
To get vendor recommendations for a given item or category, use the vendor recommendation engine at **Purchasing > Vendor Recommendations**. It analyzes:
- Historical purchase price and volume
- Average lead time
- Quality ratings from past receipts (based on rejection/return rate)
- On-time delivery percentage

Recommendations are re-scored nightly; recent data may take up to 24 hours to reflect.

## Updating Vendor Payment Terms
1. Open the vendor record under **Purchasing > Vendors**.
2. Edit the **Payment Terms** field.
3. Changes apply only to future POs and invoices, not existing open ones.

## Vendor Performance Reports
Available at **Purchasing > Reports > Vendor Performance**, showing on-time delivery rate, defect rate, and average price variance versus contract price over a selected date range.

## Deactivating a Vendor
Vendors with no open POs or unpaid invoices can be deactivated from their record page via **Actions > Deactivate Vendor**. Deactivated vendors cannot be selected on new POs but remain visible in historical records.
